About Dr. Anuroopa P

Dr. Anuroopa P is the founder and Chief Dental Surgeon of Dr Panicker’s Dental Care in Uttarahalli, Bengaluru. A periodontist and Certified Laser Specialist from Genoa University, Italy, she focuses on laser and regenerative therapy for periodontitis, ozone therapy for peri-implantitis and oral lesions, and cosmetic dentistry that harmonises pink and white aesthetics. Her practice is ethical, patient-centred and grounded in evidence-based research.
